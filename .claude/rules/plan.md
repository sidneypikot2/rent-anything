# Plan a whole trip (2026-11-02)

Nothing else in the hero starts a trip, and the example trip moved to How a trip comes together only.

**Step 1, `/plan`** (`PlanStart`, the team's A2): where (top-level destinations with things to book), when (`DateRangeField`), who (adults, children) — nothing about the stay, which is step 2's first step.
