# Plan a whole trip (2026-11-02)

The team's guided planner (A1–A6) combined with our Path B checklist (`rent-anything-plan-whole-trip.pdf`, v4: the checklist as a stepper). RAA-250 (API), RAA-251 (web).

**A1, the home hero** (RAA-275): Plan a trip (`HomePlanButton`, the filled white pill, and again in the closing card) opens `/plan` through `usePlanTripStart` (`plan-trip-start.tsx`); beside it Book one thing (down to the five kinds) and Explore (the launch area's page). Nothing else on the home starts a trip. A place page's Plan a trip buttons (banner, section bar, Sample days, closing band, phone foot) are `PlanTripLink` (same file): the same question, with the trip the page works on (`tripHere`) picked to start with, and New trip going to `/plan?where=<place>` with the page's dates — never straight to step 1, which would make a second trip.

**One track, Trip to Review** (UX-laws review, Gap 2, 2026-11-11; the user: Transfer and Vehicle stay apart, and the track shows on step 1 too): every page of the planner shows the same track (`PlanSteps`, its stops from `plan-track.ts`) — Trip (step 1), the checklist's steps, Review — and no page says "Step N of 3". On step 1 it is `PREVIEW_STOPS`: every stop a place can have, Trip lit, the rest not tappable until the trip exists. On the checklist and the review it is `planStops` over `planSteps` (the place's steps and what is done, one function for both pages): Trip is ticked and links back to step 1 (`tripStepHref`), Review links to the review (`reviewStepHref`), and on the review each step links to `/plan/<area>?trip=&choose=<key>` (`choose` takes `places` too). Skipped steps (`useSkipped`, localStorage per trip) show on both.

**Step 1, `/plan`** (`PlanStart`, the team's A2): where (top-level destinations with things to book), when (`DateRangeField modal`: the calendar in a dialog, OK applies), who (adults, children) — nothing about the stay, which is step 2's first step. Next makes a guest session when signed out, creates the trip (`POST /trips`), or with `?trip=` changes it (`PATCH`, never sending `stay`, so a saved pin stays), chooses it for the tab (`chooseTrip`) and goes to `/plan/<area>?trip=<id>`. For a guest who already has trips, every Plan a trip opens `TripChoiceSheet` where it is (with `meta` and `newDetail` in the plan's words) and leaves only once they answer (`planHref`): New trip goes to `/plan`; one of theirs to `/plan/<its place>?trip=<id>` when it has dates, else `/plan?where=<its place>&trip=<id>` (step 1 filled from it; Next changes it). Its place is the destination its first item or saved place is in (`["areas"]`, walked to the top), else the one its name starts with, else the launch area.

From `sm` the open stop's segment widens (3 shares of the track to each closed stop's 1, `OPEN_SHARE`; the track's width never changes, 2026-11-10), filled in the action colour with its icon and full name, and slides between stops; on a phone each column is as wide as its label at least (`auto` columns: eight equal ones crowded "Transfer" into "Vehicle" at 360px) and the open stop shows only its icon, filled within its column, its label in bold navy.
