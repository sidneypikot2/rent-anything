# Web UI: palette and components

The palette is Sky & Forest (RAA-102; it replaced Tidal Grove): blue to sky for actions, deep forest green for trust and trips, slate neutrals, glass over photos. It is light-only and defined once, in `web/src/app/globals.css`: named colours (`cerulean`, `emerald`, `aqua`, `fern`, `mist`, `sage`, `navy`, `forest`, `sky-deep`, `sky-pale`, `sky-mist`, `mint` (ticks for trust points on dark, RAA-125), `scrim`, the `kind-*` icon hues and the `island-*` / `city-*` badge tints (RAA-130)) and the semantic tokens components use (`background`, `foreground`, `surface`, `surface-2`, `muted`, `line`, `line-2`, `line-strong`, `link`, `on-dark`, `primary`, `primary-hover`, `secondary`, `secondary-hover`, `danger`, `warning`). `/admin/ui-kit` shows the palette and every primitive, and the home page the full look (photo hero, glass, photo cards). The design system, with a usage note per token, is the Sky & Forest artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/EHtuf5t9vYx8ygEhKEZJ4Z).

- **The system** (tokens in `globals.css`; use these, never one-off values):
  - **Spacing**: Tailwind's 4px steps inside a component; between components only `gap-gutter` (cards in a grid, page side padding `px-gutter`), `gap-block` (a heading to its content), `gap-section` (section to section), `py-page-pad` (a page's own top and bottom), `p-card` (a leading card's padding), `px-inset` (`--spacing-inset`, 1.5rem, 3rem from `sm`: a banner's contents and a section sheet's padding). They grow with the window.
  - **One inset line** (RAA-125, RAA-130): banner text, every section heading and every section's cards start `inset` in from the frame's edge — a glass or paper sheet pads its contents by it, and a section on the bare photo pads itself, heading and cards together — so a heading always sits over its own cards and the card rows of all sections line up. Only frames (the hero, sheets, the closing band) reach the outer edge — except on the guest home, whose photo sections (Beyond the launch area — its other destinations, a row of links while there are three or fewer — and How a trip comes together) drop the inset and start at the frames' edge (`.page-sections.photo-flush`). A `.page-sections` page has no side padding of its own (the banner and `PageSheet` give it).
  - **Height**: two variants in `globals.css` for when the screen's height, not its width, is the limit — `short` (under 44rem tall; pair it with `max-sm:` for a short phone) and `landscape-phone` (from `sm` wide but under 30rem tall, where `sm:` alone would give a desktop layout). A third, `fold` (under 47.5rem, a laptop window), tightens a banner's gaps. **Banners fit the first screen** (RAA-125): their height comes from their contents, never a viewport `min-h`, and `DisplayTitle` sizes by the window's height from `sm` (`min(7.2svh, 3.75rem)` for the hero) — check the hero's foot at 1366×650 and the search on a 667px-tall phone. The home hero uses them to keep its search on the first screen; a grid track that holds a scrolling row needs `minmax(0, …)`, or the row's full width stretches it.
  - **Widths**: `max-w-page` for browsing pages, `max-w-narrow` for one-thing pages (a listing, the cart, a form), `max-w-prose` for running text, `max-w-lede` for the short paragraph under a big title.
  - **Radius**: `rounded-control` (12px: buttons, fields, icon wells, glass inside a card), `rounded-card` (24px: cards, dialogs, menus), `rounded-frame` (32px: bands, banner frames, the header), `rounded-full` (pills, chips). Inner radius = outer minus the inset.
  - **Borders**: 1px only. `card-edge` (globals.css: a frosted glass rim plus a faint outline) on every card-like surface — `Card`, `CardLink`, photo cards, `Dialog`, menus, dropdowns and popovers — never a one-off border on one — except on a white sheet, where the rim doesn't show: there cards take `card-edge-line` (`Card edge="line"`, a solid `line` border; the cart, RAA-135); `border-line` for dividers inside white, `border-line-strong` on fields, `border-glass-edge` on glass; fills on glass are `bg-glass-fill` / `hover:bg-glass-hover`.
  - **Elevation**: `shadow-soft` (cards at rest), `shadow-lift` (hover, open menus), `shadow-float` (dialogs, things over a photo).
  - **Hierarchy**: one `DisplayTitle size="hero"` on the site; a page's h1 is `DisplayTitle`; every section is `Section` (`src/components/ui/section.tsx`: eyebrow, `SectionTitle`, intro, then content one `block` below), and on a `.page-sections` page it names its `surface`: `photo` (the bare backdrop), `glass` (the faint frame glass) or `paper` (a pale sheet, for a section whose content is white cards, like What to book) — never by position, which breaks when sections move (a section without one still takes the old odd/even turn). Guest browse sections go without eyebrows (RAA-125); card titles `text-lg font-semibold`; meta `text-sm text-muted`.
  - **Motion**: calm and slow, always `ease-calm`. 300ms for state changes, 500ms for hover lifts (`motion-safe:hover:-translate-y-1`: a hover lift or arrow nudge is always `motion-safe:`, as the reduced-motion rule only drops its easing and it would still jump), 700–900ms for arrivals (`animate-rise`, `animate-float-in`, staggered with `stagger-1`…`4`), `reveal` for a section easing in on scroll (never one with an `id`: a link jumping to it would land 14px low, under the search bar), `animate-drift` only for a banner photo. Never hide content at rest, except a card's details (RAA-126, below); `prefers-reduced-motion` stops all movement but keeps colour and shadow fades (globals.css).
- **Build from `src/components/ui/`**: `Button` / `ButtonLink`, `Card` / `CardBody`, `Badge`, `Pill`, `Field`, `PasswordField`, `SelectField`, `ComboboxField`, `PhoneField`, `TextareaField`, `CheckboxField`, `ChoiceCards` (radio cards with a description each), `StepperField` (a count with − and +), `DateRangeField` (one field opening a react-day-picker calendar, styled through its `DayButton` and `classNames` rather than its stylesheet, for a range or, with `single`, one day — not two `type="date"` inputs), `Dialog`, `Toast`, `Section`, `GlassPanel` (frosted glass over a photo, tones `dark` / `card` / `forest` / `light` / `frame`; `glassClasses()` for a link), `DisplayTitle`, `SectionTitle`, `Eyebrow`, `Price`. Extend a primitive (a new variant, a new prop) rather than restyling one at its call site. A new primitive also goes on `/admin/ui-kit`.
- **`Card` clips its content** (`overflow-hidden`): a `Menu` or other dropdown inside one needs `<Card clip={false}>`, or it is cut off at the card's edge (RAA-68).
- **Business cards** (team decision, 2026-10-20): What to book lists one `BusinessRow` (`src/components/discovery/business-row.tsx`) per business per group, not one row per listing. Its side panel lists that business's listings in the group, each with Add to trip, and a title swaps the same panel to that listing's details (`ListingPanel`, "← <business>" back) — never a second dialog or a page change; a business with one listing there opens its details at once and its card keeps that listing's Add to trip (with more, See N options takes that slot, so every card ends in an action). Filters and a distance search apply per listing (`ShownOffersContext`), counts are businesses, and a card lights all its pins (`MapHoverTarget` takes a list).
- **Details in a side panel** (RAA-126): on the area page a place to see (`LandmarkCard`) and a listing row (`ListingRow`) open their whole details in `Dialog placement="side"` — a floating panel on the right: the photo first, the title under it with the details, all scrolling, a floating close button and a pinned foot (price, Add to trip) — through `DetailPanelButton` (`src/components/discovery/detail-panel.tsx`), whose title button stretches over the card; the card's own controls sit above it with `relative z-10`, and nothing else on the card may be `relative`, or it swallows the click. A listing's panel fetches `GET /listings/{id}` for what the area API doesn't send. A click beside the panel closes it.
- **Area map**: an area page's sections under the search bar sit in `AreaMapLayout` (`src/components/discovery/area-map-layout.tsx`): from lg, with the map on (the rail's Show map switch; off by default and remembered per browser), the sections keep to the left and `AreaMap` sticks to the right at `--chrome`, all within `max-w-page` so they line up with the banner and search bar; What to book's rail then folds into its chips bar (`BesideMapContext`) and the map's own Hide map turns it off. Below lg a Map button opens it full screen. `AreaMapLayout` loads `AreaMap` with Next's `dynamic()` only once the map shows, so MapLibre stays out of the page's first load — never import `area-map.tsx` directly. Pins come from the explore response (`area-map-pins.ts`): a listing in its group's `pin-*` colour (tokens for pins and their key only, never a button) with its category icon and its price once it has one, places to see in navy, and listings on one rounded point as a stack that opens into a ring (a stack merged only because its points overlap at this zoom zooms in instead; one of more than six opens as a list over the map). Places to see show their names from zoom 12. A pin's pop-up opens in the middle of the map without moving it, and its title opens the same side panel as the row. Cards and rows share hover and selection with their pins through `AreaMapProvider` (`MapHoverTarget`), and What to book tells the map its group and filtered-out rows. `ListingRow` lays out by its container (`@container` on its list item), since the map can take a third of the window.
- **Confirm with `Dialog`**: a step that needs confirming (saving an edit, deleting) opens `Dialog`, with the confirming button last and `variant="danger"` for a destructive one — never `window.confirm` or a one-off modal. A step in a flow (choosing a trip, editing one) is `Dialog placement="sheet"`, docked to the bottom on a phone. The whole of a record on a work screen (the admin booking panel, RAA-142) is `Dialog placement="overlay"`: centred and wider, the title and actions fixed and the body scrolling. Every centred dialog and sheet has a close button beside its title, which takes focus first, so a sheet never opens on a field with the keyboard up. A note that something happened, which nothing waits for, is a `Toast`. On a phone a toast takes an area page's floating dock's place while it shows (`data-toast`), never stacked above it.
- **No raw colours** in components: no hex values and no Tailwind default palette classes (`gray-*`, `blue-*`, `slate-*`). If a colour is missing, add a token in `globals.css`.
- **Buttons are pills** (RAA-125): filled ones lit from above (a light top edge and a shadow in their own colour), rising 1px on hover (with a 1px strip under it while lifted, so a pointer on its bottom edge doesn't set it jittering) and pressing in on click; `arrow` adds a trailing arrow in a round well for a button that takes you somewhere; `tint` (aqua under navy, blue on hover) is a quiet action on white, led by a plus in a white disc (Add to trip).
- **Buttons are all blue**: `primary`, `secondary` (a deeper blue), `accent` and `tint` (pale `aqua` under navy) are shades of one hue, so a row of actions never mixes colours. Green (`emerald`, `forest`) marks trust and trips — badges, the trip header, bands (the home page's closing band is forest over a photo, with a blue button on it, RAA-130) — never a button. Don't alternate colours across a row of like items (tiles, cards).
- **Booking-group hues** (RAA-130): the home hero's kinds of things to book (`HeroCategories`: a quiet text row of links under an "Or browse <destination>:" label — no outline or fill, so the search's button stays the one main action — which open What to book on their group, `?show=`, at the destination picked in Where — the launch area when Where holds none — with the search's dates and guests, shared through `HeroTrip`, which also gives the home page's destination and activity cards the dates via `HeroTripLink`) are named by their group's `short` word and colour only their icon, in their What to book group's `kind-*` token (`BOOKING_GROUPS` and `GROUP_ICON` in `categories.ts`, which also name the area page's groups and the map's key — never write a group's name out elsewhere) — the map pins' `pin-*` hues lightened for dark glass, so a Tours pill and a tour's pin agree. Wayfinding, not brand: never a button, a fill or text. A destination's kind badge is `Badge tone="island"` or `"city"` on its photo card. An area page's What to book holds the listings of the places inside it too, each row naming its place (`in Santa Fe`), so a shortcut lands on the whole island; the banner's chips link to those places' own pages.
- **Page chrome**: `SectionHeader` is a sticky frosted bar (content scrolls under it; `html` has `scroll-pt-(--chrome)` so anchors clear it) with its links on glass pills. Every `PageBanner` but the home hero (which holds the search itself, `HeroSearch`: from `sm` the route card in the hero; on a phone the search bar's pill as a summary (`SearchPill summary`: no magnifier, a chevron) with the search's own button under it, full width (Start your trip, or Search opening the sheet), so the phone's first screen has one main action, and the pill opening `SearchBox hero sheet` in a sheet that stays mounted while closed, so the pill shows what was picked — one search mounted at a time, as each reports to `HeroTrip`; on a laptop's short window (`fold`) the example trip beside it drops its items' second lines, so it never stands taller than the search, RAA-130 — and leads with a trip: Where opens on the launch area (emptied, its placeholder names it and the trip still starts there), its list before typing is the destinations only — the kinds of things to book are `HeroCategories`' alone, and a search that finds nothing offers the same `BOOKING_GROUPS` — and its button is Start your trip at a destination or a thing to book (on its own row there), Search for anything else and always Search for partners and admins; on a short window the calendar and guests first move the card up under the header so they open in view; the one line under it, `TripStart`, says what the button does) and the cart (`search={false}`) has a search (RAA-126), in the header, not under the banner: `PageBanner` renders `SearchBar`, which only marks the page (`data-search-bar`, so CSS shows the header's search from the first paint, and `SearchHere` for the header once it runs), and `SectionHeader` holds `HeaderSearch` (`src/components/landing/search-bar.tsx`): a `SearchPill` filled in from the URL's dates and guests and the page's place (`<CurrentPlace name>`, rendered by the page). From the `search` breakpoint it sits between the logo and the cart and opens `SearchBox bar` as a panel under the header (closed by Escape, a click outside, or scrolling on); on a phone it is the header's second row and opens the same search in a sheet. Nothing about the search sits between the banner and the page. Picking a suggestion only fills Where and moves on to the dates, then the guests; its Search goes to the picked place with them, and Search with the place unchanged applies new dates and guests to the page itself; anything else typed (the stop is "Where or what") searches a place's What to book — on a place's page (or a listing's) that place's; on the home hero its one match, or `/search` when it matched several or none (`hrefFor`; the phone's Search goes the same way, and a Search pressed before the suggestions came fetches them first) — unless it exactly names a destination or a place to see, which then opens (`exactMatch`) (`/<area>?q=…#listings`: `ListingBrowser query` narrows every section, hides sections and chips with no match, and says it back with Search all places and Clear search), its suggestions on a place's page leading with that search and ending on "Search all places". The search never changes a trip: a trip's dates and guests change in the cart (`EditTripSheet`), and an add dated outside a trip stretches it (the server's). A search that finds nothing, in the suggestions or on /search, offers the places there are and the launch area's groups (`NoMatches`, `src/components/discovery/no-matches.tsx`); /search groups things to book by top-most destination (launch area first), each group linking to that place's What to book with the search, and shows a listing's own cover or its icon row, never a stand-in. On a place's page Where's list starts, while it holds nothing typed (or still the page's name), with "In your trip": the shown trip's other places, each going straight there on the trip's dates (`tripPlaceItems`). `--chrome` is how far down the header reaches plus 12px (estimated in `globals.css`, measured by `HeaderSearch`); anything else sticking below it uses it, never a fixed `top-20` — What to book's chips on a phone reach 20px higher, under the header's foot, so no row shows through between them. A main page opens with `PageBanner` (`src/components/landing/page-banner.tsx`: photo, scrim, title on a glass frame), which starts 12px under the header rather than sliding beneath it (RAA-125) and can hold an `aside` card from `xl` (the home hero's: the example trip from How it works, which then shows it only below `xl`; at `lg` it squeezed the search). Every page in all three sections (guest, partner, admin) sits on the fixed photo backdrop (`SiteBackdrop`) with its content on `PageSheet`: the glass sheet for guest pages, a white one (`tone="paper"`, RAA-120) for partner and admin pages (RAA-142), which are work screens — inside it, use white-surface styles (`quiet` buttons, `bg-mist` skeletons, `border-line`), never glass, except in the dark `ActionBar`. The partner and admin layouts wrap their pages in it once. The guest footer is a forest photo band with glass.
- **Cards**: `Card`, edged with `card-edge`. Things with a picture — destinations, activities, landmarks, listings (the home page's From partners, one per kind, rows on a phone; `stacked` there from `sm`: the partner's photo over the text on the card's own navy, not behind glass, so real listings read apart from the sample-photo cards; a listing with no cover passes no `photo` and is the short icon-led row) — are photo cards, navy under the photo while it loads (`Card photo`), never a white slab — a `ListingCard`'s own photo shows its category icon there while it loads (storage can hang), and keeps it if the photo fails — never a stand-in photo, which would show a listing as something it isn't (`PhotoCardFrame` `placeholder`); a photo that errors or is still loading after 8s marks the card `data-photo-missing`, and a `ListingCard` then drops the photo's room and its glass and is a short row — with the text on the denser `card` glass, which reads over any photo (`DestinationCard`/`ActivityCard` with `photo`, `LandmarkCard`, `ListingCard` in `src/components/discovery/cards.tsx`); all but `ActivityCard` are `PhotoCardFrame` (`photo-card.tsx`, RAA-126): one fixed height, a title and one line plus a foot at rest, and the rest (descriptions, tags, sub-areas, summaries) folded behind its info button (or keyboard focus inside it) — never opened on hover, so a pointer crossing a grid doesn't set cards moving. Title, price and the action never fold. On a paper sheet a listing is a `ListingRow` instead (RAA-125): its photos in `ListingPhotos` (swipe, arrows on hover, dots, "n / N"), the title, a category badge and who offers it, `listingFeatures` chips (`mist` badges, cancellation in `emerald` and first; only what its details say) kept to two rows with "+N more" (chips past it leave the flow, so none shows after it) and the summary to one line, so every row is one height (`ListingRowDetails`, RAA-126; a phone-wide row (`@max-sm`) drops the summary and keeps one category badge; no Details toggle — the listing page has the rest), then its price and Add to trip. Rows align to the top, so titles line up whatever the summaries. An area page's banner is `AreaHero` (`src/components/trips/area-hero.tsx`), and it is always the place's: the guest picks a place, adds what they need from What to book and books it from the cart — a place's page never becomes a trip's own page (the simple flow, 2026-10-22: no trip page, plan or checklist on the area page). Photo, name, counts, then the trip's one line at its foot on forest glass: with no trip here (signed out, none yet, loading) how the trip starts (with the first add) and a link down to What to book (compact, one slim row, `trip-lead-compact`, so a phone's first screen always has a way to something to book); once the guest has a current trip with something here (an item in this place or one inside it), or chose one to work on (`chosen-trip.ts`, even with nothing here yet; `useTripHere`), `TripNotice` — its name, dates, what it has here and elsewhere, and Checkout, a filled blue button and the banner's one main action while anything in it can be booked (View trip, quiet, once all of it is booked), which opens it in the cart (`/cart?trip=<id>`; compact, one slim row with its name). A guest with more than one trip switches here: Switch trip (or, with trips but none shown, Choose a trip) opens `TripChoiceSheet`, the home page's Which trip? (given the page's `places`, the trips with something here come first, under "In <place>"); a trip puts its own dates and guests in the URL (`tripOwnQuery`) and becomes the add sheet's preselected trip, New trip shows the lead. It only sets the default: every add still confirms its trip. Under its name the trip's places (`tripPlaces`, from each item's `destination`; a trip stores none) are chips: this page's marked (while the trip has something here, compact, it reads "Edit" and opens; wide, the rows above it do, so the chip only marks it — `HereItemsSheet`: the trip's items here, each with Change date (`ItemDateSheet`) and Remove; a booked one only links to the trip, and moving stays in the cart), each other a `prefetch` link to its page on the trip's own dates (`tripOwnQuery`), and "+ Another place" opening the search on Where; compact, only for a trip in more than one place or with something here, in a sideways row under the slim one. So a trip in Bantayan and Cebu City goes from one to the other in one tap, with no search each way and no place dates. Under them, what the trip still lacks (`missingHere` in `trip-missing.ts`): nights ahead with no stay or too few beds, and no transfer here, each only when the place has that group, each linking to its section (`#need-stay`); compact, the slim row says "needs a stay" in place of its count. A hint, never a step to finish: tours, rentals and gear are the guest's choice, never missing, and a day trip has no nights to fill. The header's search owns the page's dates; a trip's own dates change in the cart. The server renders the signed-out state and the trip's line swaps in after the session loads, in the same frame (one `PageBanner`, so the photo stays mounted). On a phone or a short window (`max-search` / `fold`, chosen in CSS) the wide lead gives way to the notice's slim row, so the first screen still reaches What to book; check both states at 390×667 and 1366×650. Which section leads is `SearchedFirst`'s, decided on arrival: What to book when the URL came with a search (dates, `?show=`, `?q=`), the places to see for an open-ended start; dates the page fills in itself never reorder it. A `?show=` or `?q=` arrival scrolls to What to book from `ListingBrowser` once laid out (not the browser's own jump), and a `?show=` says "Showing <group> on <place>" with Show everything. What to book is a `ListingBrowser`: a filter rail from `lg` sticking at `--chrome`, under the header (the sections with their counts in one right-aligned column, then Show map), chips on a phone, sticking under the header. Priced rows lead each section; an unpriced row says "Price to be agreed" (`TO_BE_AGREED`) and its section says once, under its title, what that means (`pricedByNote`), never the full sentence on every row; a chip every listing in a section has (Free cancellation, say) goes in that note as "All of these include: …" instead of on each card (`sharedChipsOf`, `BusinessRow sharedChips`; the panel still lists it); a row the trip being worked on holds (the chosen trip, `useListingInTrip`; with no choice, any trip) shows `InTripBadge` and its button becomes a quieter Add another, while a row only another trip holds says "In another trip" and keeps Add to trip. Under All a section shows its first three rows and "Show all N", which picks it; a linked row past them picks its section; the banner's counts link down to What to book and Places to see (its "to book" counts the same cards as All, never listings; the home page's destinations and the search's destination suggestions say the same number from the API's `to_book_count`, through `countLine`). A place to see gets a stand-in photo only when one fits it and no other place on the page has it (`landmarkPhotos`), marked "Sample photo" where it shows, otherwise its icon; its card links to the rows that go there (`visited_by`), and What to book shows a linked row (`#listing-<id>`) whatever section is picked. Every shown section keeps its title, with a filter button (`FilterGlyph`, a count badge while set) opening that section's filters in a `Dialog` (free cancellation, a price range on "from" prices, what the rows include; applied on Show N results), and what is set beside it as removable pills kept to one row, the rest counted in "+N more" (`useFitRows`, shared with the row's chips). Filters belong to one section (RAA-126), so a price set for a rental never hides a stay.
- **Fields and badges**: fields are 48px (`min-h-12`), `rounded-xl`, `px-4`, `line-strong` border, focus `border-primary` + `ring-4 ring-sky-mist`; labels `text-sm font-semibold`. Badges are 28px pills in sentence case, 13px semibold — never uppercase.
- **Glass only over a photo**: a photo with text on it gets a scrim (`bg-linear-to-b from-scrim/.. to-scrim/..`); controls on it are `GlassPanel`. White text on dark or forest glass, `sky-mist` for its muted text, `sky-pale` for accents. On white, use a `Card`, never glass.
- **Sample photos**: destinations and activities have no photos in the API yet, so the home page uses stand-ins from `src/components/landing/sample-photos.ts` (`public/images/samples/`, alt text says "Sample photo"). Replace them when the API serves real ones. Listings have their own photos (RAA-109, `web-photos.md`); their stand-in is only the fallback.
- **No emoji as icons**: line icons on a 24px grid, 1.8 stroke, `currentColor`, from `src/components/discovery/tag-glyphs.tsx` (`TagGlyph`, `AreaGlyph`, `CategoryGlyph`, `PlaceGlyph`; `icons.tsx` wraps them at text size). A new icon goes there. An icon leading a row sits in a `size-11 rounded-xl bg-mist text-primary` well.
- **Contrast pairs**:
  - white on `primary` (5.2:1), `secondary` (8.7:1), `emerald` (7.1:1) or `forest`;
  - navy on `aqua` or `fern`, never white;
  - `on-dark`, white or `aqua` on `navy`;
  - `muted` for secondary text on white or `surface-2`;
  - `line` is for card borders only; form fields need `line-strong` (3:1 against white).
- **Type**: one family, Plus Jakarta Sans, for both `font-display` and `font-sans`. Sizes are Tailwind's steps plus four in `globals.css` — `text-small` (13px: badges, pin labels, card meta), `text-control` (15px: buttons, fields), `text-title-sm` (17px: a small card's title), `text-band` (the closing band's title) — never a one-off `text-[…]`. Headings are `font-display font-extrabold tracking-tight`, upright — no italic headings. The one italic is the hero's closing phrase on a photo, in `sky-pale`.
- **Classes don't merge**: `cn()` only joins class names, so a `className` passed to a primitive adds to its classes and can't reliably override one on the same property.
- **Brand**: the product name and tagline come from `web/src/lib/brand.ts` (`BRAND`, `BRAND_PARTS` for the two-tone wordmark in `Logo`, `TAGLINE`) — never write them out in a component or in page metadata (RAA-35). The repo, API and docs keep Rent-Anything. The smoke test (`tools/smoke/tests/smoke.spec.js`) is the one place outside `brand.ts` that spells the name, so a rename updates it too.
- **Booking copy** (one voice, `BOOKING_STATE` in `launch-area.ts`): a trip's items go to their partners as requests and nothing is paid in the app yet. Never "booking opens later" (requests already go out) or "nothing is charged until the partner accepts".
- **First add, signed out**: the sheet (`SignInSheet`) leads with Create a free account (`trip-signin-create`, to `/register?next=`); signing in follows in an `AuthCard` with its own `title` and no switch link. The header's account button is the first letter below `sm`, so a signed-in phone header stays one row.
