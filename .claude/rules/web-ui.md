# Web UI: palette and components

The palette is Sky & Forest (RAA-102; it replaced Tidal Grove): blue to sky for actions, deep forest green for trust and trips, slate neutrals, glass over photos. It is light-only and defined once, in `web/src/app/globals.css`: named colours (`cerulean`, `emerald`, `aqua`, `fern`, `mist`, `sage`, `navy`, `forest`, `sky-deep`, `sky-pale`, `sky-mist`, `mint` (ticks for trust points on dark, RAA-125), `scrim`, the `kind-*` icon hues and the `island-*` / `city-*` badge tints (RAA-130)) and the semantic tokens components use (`background`, `foreground`, `surface`, `surface-2`, `muted`, `line`, `line-2`, `line-strong`, `link`, `on-dark`, `primary`, `primary-hover`, `secondary`, `secondary-hover`, `danger`, `warning`). `/admin/ui-kit` shows the palette and every primitive, and the home page the full look (photo hero, glass, photo cards). The design system, with a usage note per token, is the Sky & Forest artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/EHtuf5t9vYx8ygEhKEZJ4Z).

- **The system** (tokens in `globals.css`; use these, never one-off values):
  - **Spacing**: Tailwind's 4px steps inside a component; between components only `gap-gutter` (cards in a grid, page side padding `px-gutter`), `gap-block` (a heading to its content), `gap-section` (section to section), `py-page-pad` (a page's own top and bottom), `p-card` (a leading card's padding), `px-inset` (`--spacing-inset`, 1.5rem, 3rem from `sm`: a banner's contents and a section sheet's padding). They grow with the window.
  - **One inset line** (RAA-125, RAA-130): banner text, every section heading and every section's cards start `inset` in from the frame's edge — a glass or paper sheet pads its contents by it, and a section on the bare photo pads itself, heading and cards together — so a heading always sits over its own cards and the card rows of all sections line up. Only frames (the hero, sheets, the closing band) reach the outer edge — except on the guest home, whose photo sections (Beyond the launch area — its other destinations, a row of links while there are three or fewer — and How a trip comes together) drop the inset and start at the frames' edge (`.page-sections.photo-flush`). A `.page-sections` page has no side padding of its own (the banner and `PageSheet` give it).
  - **Height**: two variants in `globals.css` for when the screen's height, not its width, is the limit — `short` (under 44rem tall; pair it with `max-sm:` for a short phone) and `landscape-phone` (from `sm` wide but under 30rem tall, where `sm:` alone would give a desktop layout). A third, `fold` (under 47.5rem, a laptop window), tightens a banner's gaps. **Banners fit the first screen** (RAA-125): their height comes from their contents, never a viewport `min-h`, and `DisplayTitle` sizes by the window's height from `sm` (`min(7.2svh, 3.75rem)` for the hero) — check the hero's foot at 1366×650 and the search on a 667px-tall phone. The home hero uses them to keep its search on the first screen; a grid track that holds a scrolling row needs `minmax(0, …)`, or the row's full width stretches it.
  - **Widths**: `max-w-page` for browsing pages, `max-w-narrow` for one-thing pages (a listing, the cart, a form), `max-w-prose` for running text, `max-w-lede` for the short paragraph under a big title.
  - **Radius**: on guest pages everything pressed is a pill — `rounded-button` and `rounded-chip` are fully round there (10px on partner and admin pages): buttons, chips, tabs, steppers, link tiles; `rounded-control` (12px: fields, icon wells, the desktop rail's rows, glass inside a card), `rounded-card` (24px: cards, dialogs, menus), `rounded-frame` (32px: bands, banner frames, the header). Inner radius = outer minus the inset.
  - **Borders**: 1px only. `card-edge` (globals.css: a frosted glass rim plus a faint outline) on every card-like surface — `Card`, `CardLink`, photo cards, `Dialog`, menus, dropdowns and popovers — never a one-off border on one — except on a white sheet, where the rim doesn't show: there cards take `card-edge-line` (`Card edge="line"`, a solid `line` border; the cart, RAA-135); `border-line` for dividers inside white, `border-line-strong` on fields, `border-glass-edge` on glass; fills on glass are `bg-glass-fill` / `hover:bg-glass-hover`.
  - **Elevation**: `shadow-soft` (cards at rest), `shadow-lift` (hover, open menus), `shadow-float` (dialogs, things over a photo).
  - **Hierarchy**: one `DisplayTitle size="hero"` on the site; a page's h1 is `DisplayTitle`; every section is `Section` (`src/components/ui/section.tsx`: eyebrow, `SectionTitle`, intro, then content one `block` below), and on a `.page-sections` page it names its `surface`: `photo` (the bare backdrop), `glass` (the faint frame glass) or `paper` (a pale sheet, for a section whose content is white cards, like What to book) — never by position, which breaks when sections move (a section without one still takes the old odd/even turn). Guest browse sections go without eyebrows (RAA-125); card titles `text-lg font-semibold`; meta `text-sm text-muted`.
  - **Motion**: calm and slow, always `ease-calm`, except `ease-soft` (a slow start and stop) for what moves the whole page, which `ease-calm`'s quick start makes uncomfortable (the partner rail, RAA-267). 300ms for state changes, 500ms for hover lifts (`motion-safe:hover:-translate-y-1`: a hover lift or arrow nudge is always `motion-safe:`, as the reduced-motion rule only drops its easing and it would still jump), 700–900ms for arrivals (`animate-rise`, `animate-float-in`, staggered with `stagger-1`…`4`), `reveal` for a section easing in on scroll (never one with an `id`: a link jumping to it would land 14px low, under the search bar), `animate-drift` only for a banner photo, a photo carousel's turn a 700ms slide that a finger's swipe drags along (`BannerCarousel`, `web-photos.md`), `animate-swap-in` (0.4s, a fade and 8px rise) for What to book's rows when another section is picked, 60ms apart and never on the first paint. Never hide content at rest, except a card's details (RAA-126, below); `prefers-reduced-motion` stops all movement but keeps colour and shadow fades (globals.css).
- **Build from `src/components/ui/`**: `Button` / `ButtonLink`, `Card` / `CardBody`, `Badge`, `Pill`, `Field`, `PasswordField`, `SelectField`, `ComboboxField`, `PhoneField`, `TextareaField`, `CheckboxField`, `ChoiceCards` (radio cards with a description each), `StepperField` (a count with − and +), `DateRangeField` (one field opening a react-day-picker calendar, styled through its `DayButton` and `classNames` rather than its stylesheet, for a range or, with `single`, one day — not two `type="date"` inputs), `Dialog`, `Toast`, `Section`, `GlassPanel` (frosted glass over a photo, tones `dark` / `card` / `forest` / `light` / `frame`; `glassClasses()` for a link), `DisplayTitle`, `SectionTitle`, `Eyebrow`, `Price`. Extend a primitive (a new variant, a new prop) rather than restyling one at its call site. A new primitive also goes on `/admin/ui-kit`.
- **`Card` clips its content** (`overflow-hidden`): a `Menu` or other dropdown inside one needs `<Card clip={false}>`, or it is cut off at the card's edge (RAA-68).

- **Details in a side panel** (RAA-126): a place to see (`LandmarkCard`, its map pop-up) opens its whole details in `Dialog placement="side"` — a floating panel on the right: the photo first, the title under it with the details, all scrolling, a floating close button and a pinned foot (price, Add to trip) — through `DetailPanelButton` (`src/components/discovery/detail-panel.tsx`), whose title button stretches over the card; the card's own controls sit above it with `relative z-10`, and nothing else on the card may be `relative`, or it swallows the click. A listing never opens one (it opens its page, above). On a phone `placement="side"` fills the screen with a back arrow in the close disc's place, as a page sliding in; from `sm` it floats on the right.
- **Area map**: an area page's sections under the search bar sit in `AreaMapLayout` (`src/components/discovery/area-map-layout.tsx`): from lg, with the map on (the rail's Show map switch; off by default and remembered per browser), the sections keep to the left and `AreaMap` sticks to the right at `--chrome`, all within `max-w-page` so they line up with the banner and search bar; What to book's rail then folds into its chips bar (`BesideMapContext`) and the map's own Hide map turns it off. Below lg a Map button opens it full screen. `AreaMapLayout` loads `AreaMap` with Next's `dynamic()` only once the map shows, so MapLibre stays out of the page's first load — never import `area-map.tsx` directly. Pins come from the explore response (`area-map-pins.ts`): a listing in its group's `pin-*` colour (tokens for pins and their key only, never a button) with its category icon and its price once it has one, places to see in navy, and listings on one rounded point as a stack that opens into a ring (a stack merged only because its points overlap at this zoom zooms in instead; one of more than six opens as a list over the map). Places to see show their names from zoom 12. A pin's pop-up opens in the middle of the map without moving it, and its title opens the listing's page (a place to see's, its side panel). Cards and rows share hover and selection with their pins through `AreaMapProvider` (`MapHoverTarget`), and What to book tells the map its group and filtered-out rows. `ListingRow` lays out by its container (`@container` on its list item), since the map can take a third of the window.
- **Map markers** (RAA-256): a `Marker`'s element is MapLibre's: it places it with the `maplibregl-marker` classes and a `transform`. Set no `className` on it once it is a marker and give it no `transform` transition, or the pin leaves its point and floats. Anything that restyles a pin goes on a child inside it (`results-map.tsx`, `area-map.tsx`). A guest's map stays flat with north up: `FLAT_MAP` in its options and `keepMapFlat(map)` after it (`web/src/lib/map.ts`).
- **One dialog anatomy** (the modal redesign, 2026-10-28, `rent-anything-modal-design.pdf`, direction A): every `Dialog` is a grab handle on a phone (drag down closes; docked from the bottom with 32px top corners, a centred card from `sm`), a header that names the thing — the listing, the trip, the question — never the action (`title`, a one-line `meta`, a 64px `thumb`), the body, and an action bar pinned to its foot: an optional `summary` (`DialogSummary`: a total or what the button does) on the left and the actions on the right. No Cancel button beside a non-destructive action: the close disc, a drag down, Escape or a click outside closes it. **Closing** (the team's pick, 2026-10-29, after RAA-194 had stopped outside clicks): a form holding changes passes `dirty` (`useChanged(values)` from `dialog.tsx`, or its own flag), and then every way of closing asks "Discard changes?" (Keep editing / Discard) first, as on iOS and Android; closing it yourself (`open={false}` after a save) never asks. Only a click pressed and released outside counts, so a press that slides out or a button that moves under the pointer never closes one. Passing `dirty`: the add sheet, item edit, edit / new / recreate trip, change booking, checkout's contact details, a typed cancel or decline reason, the payout account sheet and the partner calendar's forms; read-only panels, pickers, search, filters and confirmations close freely; the button says the outcome ("Start a trip", "Add to <trip>", "Add 1 more", "Save changes"). Choices are a `Ticket` (`src/components/ui/ticket.tsx`) of `TicketRow`s (an icon in a tinted well, a label, the value, a `Stepper` or a chevron; `selected` for a picker's radio) with notes in its foot (`TicketNote`); `DateRangeField row` and `RoomsAndGuests row` are ticket rows too, and `TripOption` is one. A sheet with its own `<form>` puts its submit in `DialogFooter`. A confirmation that can't be undone is `tone="danger"` (the alert disc), the safe button first ("Keep it") and the danger one last.
- **Confirm with `Dialog`**: a step that needs confirming (saving an edit, deleting) opens `Dialog`, with the confirming button last and `variant="danger"` for a destructive one — never `window.confirm` or a one-off modal. A step in a flow (choosing a trip, editing one) is `Dialog placement="sheet"`, docked to the bottom on a phone. The whole of a record on a work screen (the admin booking panel, RAA-142) is `Dialog placement="overlay"`: centred and wider, the title and actions fixed and the body scrolling. Every centred dialog and sheet has a close button beside its title, which takes focus first, so a sheet never opens on a field with the keyboard up. A note that something happened, which nothing waits for, is a `Toast`. On a phone a toast takes an area page's floating dock's place while it shows (`data-toast`), never stacked above it.
- **Seats and beds against the party**: wherever a guest picks vehicles or rooms for the trip's people (the shop panel, a stay's Rooms and guests, a mix of room types), `CoverBar` (`src/components/trips/cover-bar.tsx`) says it: what is picked, counted with what the trip already holds on those days (a shop panel adds the trip's vehicle rentals from any shop, `ridesHeld`, names the days still short, and opens on the longest run of them), "Everyone has a seat / bed" in `emerald` or "N of your M still need a ride / bed" in `warning`, and a bar filling in the action colour — never a sentence alone.
- **No raw colours** in components: no hex values and no Tailwind default palette classes (`gray-*`, `blue-*`, `slate-*`). If a colour is missing, add a token in `globals.css`.
- **Buttons** (RAA-183, after the team's soft-UI reference; guest pages — partner and admin pages keep the blue `primary`, `soft` in blue text on a hairline): pills, semibold, no lift; the main one filled in the action colour (`bg-action`) and lit softly from above (a face a touch lighter at the top, a faint bright top edge, a soft shadow in its own colour — subtle, never a glossy 3D button), a second action (`soft`: See details, Add another, Cancel, and the Filter button) outlined in the action colour — a 1px lagoon-ink edge and text on white, its tint on hover — never a white pill with only a hairline, which reads as a label on a white card, and never the pale tint without an edge, which is a tag's (`Badge kind`); `paper` (a white pill, gently lit) is the main action on a photo or a band; pressed, a button settles (its shadow goes, a light inset shade); disabled, it is a flat muted pill (`disabled`, `disabled-ink`), never a faded one; working, `loading` keeps its colour with a spinner before the label ("Adding…") — never `disabled` for a pending request, which reads as unavailable. The shadows and states are guest-only, in `globals.css` on the `btn` / `btn-<variant>` classes `Button` adds. A small icon after the label says what it does (`glyph`: plus adds to a trip, check marks it in the trip, arrow moves on — `arrow` for the last). A listing's Add to trip is the filled main pill wherever it shows (rows, panels, sheets), as the reference's card action; `tint` (the action tint under its ink, a solid 1px edge) is left for quiet adds elsewhere (the cart's). On a photo or a trip band the main button is `paper` (white).

- **Contrast pairs**:
  - white on `primary` (5.2:1), `secondary` (8.7:1), `emerald` (7.1:1) or `forest`;
  - navy on `aqua` or `fern`, never white;
  - `on-dark`, white or `aqua` on `navy`;
  - `muted` for secondary text on white or `surface-2`;
  - `line` is for card borders only; form fields need `line-strong` (3:1 against white).
- **Type**: one family, Plus Jakarta Sans, for both `font-display` and `font-sans`. Sizes are Tailwind's steps plus four in `globals.css` — `text-small` (13px: badges, pin labels, card meta), `text-control` (15px: buttons, fields), `text-title-sm` (17px: a small card's title), `text-band` (the closing band's title) — never a one-off `text-[…]`. Headings are `font-display font-extrabold tracking-tight`, upright — no italic headings. The one italic is the hero's closing phrase on a photo, in `sky-pale`.
- **Classes don't merge**: `cn()` only joins class names, so a `className` passed to a primitive adds to its classes and can't reliably override one on the same property.
